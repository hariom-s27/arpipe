# T0.4 DocumentGold: MD&A Annotation Research

**Status:** RESEARCH_ONLY. This is a boundary-method study, not DocumentGold and not a replacement for frozen ARPipe segmentation rules.
**Sources:** S01–S08 in [`research_source_register.csv`](../../research_source_register.csv); frozen-project sources are named below.
**Key caution:** regulatory requirements describe disclosure context; they do not themselves establish the physical start or end page of a particular annual report.

## What is frozen and what is not

T0.4 already fixes Track B as a full-document task with page-span IoU as its primary metric. The existing schema separates DocumentGold from PageGold and reserves fields for `mda_present`, start/end pages, start heading/level, ambiguity, neighbours, and provenance. Existing ARPipe tests and heuristics recognise many MD&A headings, pointers, combined headings, Directors' Report variants, annexures, and terminators. They are candidate-generation/processing behaviour, not a human Gold definition.

The following are therefore open: the human presence states; exact meaning of start/end at a page boundary; whether a combined or multiple MD&A arrangement has a primary span; the treatment of ambiguity in the primary denominator; and the independent/adjudicated workflow. No proposed rule below changes `configs/t0_4`, production segmentation, the frozen sampling population, or historical labels.

## Regulatory context is not boundary truth

The consolidated SEBI LODR text inspected in S08 says that Regulation 34(2)(e) includes a management discussion and analysis report in the annual report either as part of the directors report or additional to it. It also states that the 2015 regulations came into force after their Gazette publication and rescinded earlier listing-agreement circulars subject to savings. This supports two limited conclusions:

1. For a listed-entity report governed by the post-2015 LODR regime, an MD&A may be structurally embedded in, rather than necessarily separate from, a Directors' Report.
2. The source is **regulatory context**, not evidence that a named page in a given PDF belongs to MD&A.

The frozen corpus includes reports before the LODR period. Current or consolidated LODR wording must not be projected backwards as a definition for those reports. Before Gold protocol integration, historical pre-LODR requirements need their own dated official source if the protocol will make a legal-period claim. Until then the protocol should remain documentary: annotate the actual report's visible structure under the frozen human definition, record the report era, and label regulatory applicability `NOT_VERIFIED_FOR_ERA`. This avoids transforming a legal expectation into a physical boundary rule.

## Recommended raw DocumentGold record model

The boolean-shaped frozen schema is a scaffold. The following is a **research recommendation pending author approval**, expressed as a record model rather than a new final config.

| Component | Recommended content | Why it is needed |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | `document_id`, physical page convention, source-PDF SHA-256, protocol/tool/version, anonymous annotator | reproducibility and source binding |
| Presence | `PRESENT`, `ABSENT`, `AMBIGUOUS_STRUCTURE`, `NON_STANDARD_DOCUMENT`, `INPUT_UNAVAILABLE`, or `NOT_YET_ANNOTATED` | does not force a false binary where evidence is insufficient |
| Candidate evidence | all designated candidate headings/spans, visible heading text, level/parent context, occurrence kind, source-aid flags | makes a human conclusion auditable without exposing pipeline output |
| Selected span | `start_page`, `end_page`, start heading, end-boundary note, whether either page is mixed | supplies page-span IoU when an unambiguous span exists |
| Alternatives | `admissible_spans`, `primary_span_rule_status`, `ambiguity_status`, reason code | preserves genuine ambiguity and multiple-valid-interpretations |
| Neighbour context | preceding/next top-level or annexure heading, excluded neighbours, transition evidence | makes semantic start/end review possible |
| Raw vs final lineage | independent record ID, second-record link, agreement type, adjudicator ID/reason, final-reference ID | ensures adjudication never overwrites raw annotations |

`INPUT_UNAVAILABLE` is a source/input state, not an `ABSENT` MD&A judgement. The 14 such frozen Track-B documents must remain visible in a future flow and in reporting.

## Operational research recommendations

### Presence

`PRESENT` requires an actual body section whose visible heading/designation and substantive body support an MD&A interpretation under the approved guideline. A table-of-contents entry, bookmark, running header, cross-reference, or sentence saying an MD&A exists elsewhere is a discovery cue only, not a body section. `ABSENT` means a full-document review finds no qualifying body section under the protocol. `AMBIGUOUS_STRUCTURE` means evidence supports more than one defensible interpretation or lacks enough structure to decide; it is not annotator error. `NON_STANDARD_DOCUMENT` records a document whose genre does not support the intended annual-report task. `INPUT_UNAVAILABLE` has no annotation inference.

### Start page

For a qualifying standalone heading or a designated subheading inside a Directors' Report, the recommended page-span start is the **physical heading page** if the heading belongs to the actual body section. If a heading is the final visible item on a page and substantive prose starts next page, retain the heading page for a page-level span and additionally record `substantive_start_page = next_page`. This avoids quietly swapping a document-structure decision for an evaluation convenience. A TOC/pointer/bookmark-only occurrence never establishes a start. Fuzzy, bilingual, and combined wording need a captured visible string and explicit confidence/ambiguity—not a keyword-based auto-decision.

### End page

The recommended end is the final physical page containing content assigned to the qualifying MD&A section before the next top-level section/annexure/document end. If a next top-level heading starts mid-page after MD&A prose, the page remains the end page and receives `MIXED_BOUNDARY_PAGE`; page-span IoU cannot represent sub-page transition exactly. A repeated keyword, a page-count prior, or the last page containing “MD&A” is not an end rule. Tables, outlook, risk content, and Directors' Report material must be included/excluded from the span according to their demonstrated local hierarchy, not topic words alone.

### Multiple or combined MD&A sections

A regulatory placement rule does not justify selecting one of several real candidate spans. In a document with standalone, consolidated, subsidiary, repeated, combined, or annexure MD&A sections, retain every candidate and set `PRIMARY_SPAN_RULE_UNRESOLVED` unless a later frozen protocol defines a task-aligned primary selection rule. Do not invent such a rule merely to manufacture an IoU denominator. The eventual results table must show counts for unambiguous, adjudicated, ambiguous/multiple, absent, non-standard, and input-unavailable documents; it must not silently drop the difficult categories.

## Ambiguity and denominator flow

| Raw status | Independent annotation | Adjudication action | Final reference state | Primary metric handling (must freeze later) | Secondary reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ANNOTATOR_ERROR` | one record violates an operational rule | correctable by evidence-based adjudication | adjudicated span/presence with reason | can enter only under the frozen rule | count error type |
| `GUIDELINE_AMBIGUITY` | guideline does not choose between interpretations | escalate to protocol owner; do not silently settle | unresolved or versioned decision | do not alter formal reliability units post hoc | count and explain |
| `SOURCE_AMBIGUITY` | source really supports multiple structures | retain alternatives | `ADJUDICATED_REFERENCE_ANNOTATION` may remain ambiguous | report a predeclared ambiguity category; no silent exclusion | candidate and alternative span distribution |
| `MISSING_EVIDENCE` | pages/input cannot support judgement | verify source availability/rendering | `INPUT_UNAVAILABLE` or missing-evidence record | outside span calculation but in manifest accounting | count by reason |
| `MULTIPLE_VALID_INTERPRETATIONS` | different defensible spans/primary candidates | record all, do not majority-vote by default | primary-rule unresolved until approved | no invented primary IoU | counts and pairwise span similarity |
| `TECHNICAL_RENDERING_AMBIGUITY` | rendering prevents reliable reading | retain source/render identifiers | unresolved technical record | predeclare missing/reference policy | count and remediation path |

The phrase **adjudicated reference annotation** is preferable to “objective ground truth.” It describes a reviewable methodological decision while retaining the disagreement and source uncertainty that led to it.

## Non-Gold MD&A boundary casebook

The casebook uses synthetic feature descriptions only. It reads no annual report and creates no label. `artifacts/t0_4_gold_method_research/casebook_rule_check.py` exercises each case in 200 random rule orders; its current result is 14/14 order-independent, 0 rule-set defects. This establishes only internal rule consistency—not human reliability or an approved protocol.

| Case | Synthetic context / problem | Expected annotation principle | Failure risk | Rule stressed | Remaining ambiguity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CASE-01 | standalone body MD&A, next top-level page | start at heading page; end before next section | keyword-only start | body designation / page span | none in synthetic case |
| CASE-02 | MD&A subheading inside Directors' Report | qualify as embedded MD&A if body structure supports it | treating Directors' Report as automatic exclusion | embedded-heading start | factual report hierarchy still matters |
| CASE-03 | heading begins mid-page | physical heading page is start | choosing next page for convenience | start rule | none |
| CASE-04 | MD&A continues across pages | retain continuous page span | stop at heading page | continuation | none |
| CASE-05 | next top-level section begins mid-page | end page remains mixed boundary page | assigning whole page to next section | mixed-boundary end | sub-page truth is deliberately recorded, not scored as a page split |
| CASE-06 | annexure follows | stop before/at transition per visible content | annexure bleed | annexure end | may need hierarchy evidence |
| CASE-07 | multiple body headings | preserve candidates | arbitrary “first heading wins” | multiplicity | `PRIMARY_SPAN_RULE_UNRESOLVED` |
| CASE-08 | TOC lists MD&A differently from body | body controls; TOC is aid only | TOC becomes Gold | aid separation | record discrepancy |
| CASE-09 | bilingual heading | preserve visible wording and structural evidence | English-keyword false negative | bilingual flag | translation/semantics may remain uncertain |
| CASE-10 | unclear transition | record alternatives | forced exact end | ambiguity rule | explicit ambiguous transition |

Two probes were added for TOC-only and pointer-only occurrences (no start/end), one for a combined heading (explicit unresolved state), and one for a heading at page bottom (heading vs substantive start recorded separately). The checker originally exposed an incoherent no-start/end interaction; it was repaired in the research-only draft before the reported run. That is useful evidence that a guideline needs stress tests before it is used on humans.

## Context and annotation aids

DocumentGold requires whole-report access: resolving structural level, a continuation, a pointer, an annexure, and a neighbouring top-level heading cannot reliably be done from a page alone. The task interface should provide a page jump, thumbnails, visible physical page number, and inspectable immediate hierarchy. It may offer TOC/bookmark search as an explicitly logged `ANNOTATION_AID`. It must not provide ARPipe candidates, model/OCR output, benchmark condition/split, or another annotator's record. Full-document access is necessary context, not permission to use hidden computational evidence.

## Practical disagreement taxonomy

| Code | Meaning | Measurement / adjudication use |
|---|---|---|
| `START_PAGE_OFFSET` | different selected start page | exact/±1/distance reporting; inspect heading/substantive distinction |
| `END_PAGE_OFFSET` | different selected end page | span IoU/distance reporting; inspect transition hierarchy |
| `HEADING_VS_SUBSTANTIVE_START` | heading page versus prose page | protocol refinement signal |
| `DIRECTORS_REPORT_SCOPE` | embedded MD&A treatment differs | boundary/adjudication reason |
| `ANNEXURE_SCOPE` | annexure inclusion differs | boundary/adjudication reason |
| `TOC_OR_POINTER_AS_BODY` | aid was mistaken for a section | training/pilot correction |
| `MULTIPLE_CANDIDATES` | primary selection differs | preserve alternatives; do not force vote |
| `BILINGUAL_OR_FUZZY_HEADING` | designation evidence differs | record language/evidence limitation |
| `MIXED_BOUNDARY_PAGE` | sub-page transition drives disagreement | page-span limitation audit |
| `UNREADABLE_OR_MISSING_EVIDENCE` | source cannot support a decision | source/technical disposition |
| `GUIDELINE_OR_EXECUTION_ERROR` | ambiguity in rules versus an application mistake | separates protocol improvement from coder correction |

## Findings that require later author approval

- Presence-state vocabulary and its mapping to the eventual boolean schema.
- Whether genuinely ambiguous/multiple cases are outside the primary page-span calculation, represented by a predeclared alternate metric, or assigned an author-approved primary rule.
- The exact visible-heading rule for non-standard/bilingual MD&A naming and whether specialist language review is required.
- The end-page policy for a mixed transition page and the secondary `substantive_start_page` field.
- The historical-regulatory evidence needed if the thesis makes era-specific legal assertions.

None of these findings decides the legacy-font route, route precedence, or OracleRouteGold evidence policy.
