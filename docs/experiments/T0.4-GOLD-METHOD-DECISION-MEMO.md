## Direct answers for protocol integration

1. **Minimum PageGold information:** a visual reference transcription/text-status for each page in the declared CER denominator; page/source provenance; and only typed fields consumed by retained structure/secondary metrics.
2. **Minimum DocumentGold information:** source-bound presence state, candidate and selected/admissible span evidence, start/end/neighbour structure, ambiguity, and raw/adjudicated provenance.
3. **Fields requiring double annotation:** all reliability-critical human judgements in the selected formal overlap: text, anchor/order, retained headings/facts, and DocumentGold states/spans. Server-generated provenance is mechanically validated instead.
4. **Defensible overlap:** precommitted hybrid random reliability overlap plus an explicitly separate rare/difficult diagnostic layer. No Page/Document percentage is frozen.
5. **Reading-order unit:** proposed atomic visual text region, not an engine block/line identifier; pending B7 approval.
6. **Reading-order relation:** directed `PRECEDES` relation over eligible page-local anchor pairs; no implicit order for excluded/tied/missing items.
7. **Agreement measure per task:** see the agreement matrix; no one statistic fits transcription, intervals, categorical states, relations, and set-valued facts.
8. **Agreement uncertainty:** numerator/denominator, point estimate, predeclared 95% bootstrap interval, clusters, missingness, and distance/IoU distributions.
9. **MD&A presence:** qualifying body structure, not a keyword/TOC/pointer; retain ambiguous/non-standard/input-unavailable states.
10. **MD&A start:** physical qualifying-heading page, with substantive-start-next-page recorded when applicable.
11. **MD&A end:** last qualifying-content page before visible structural transition; record a mixed boundary page.
12. **Multiple MD&A:** preserve candidates; no ungrounded primary selection. `PRIMARY_SPAN_RULE_UNRESOLVED` is an acceptable scientific outcome.
13. **Ambiguity:** source, guideline, missing-evidence, multiple-valid-interpretation, technical-rendering, annotator-error states travel from raw record through adjudication and reporting.
14. **Adjudication:** expert evidence-based review after independent work; escalation for source/rule ambiguity; never erase raw disagreement.
15. **Original disagreement:** append-only raw A/B plus linked taxonomy/reason/final record; never calculate agreement from adjudicated labels.
16. **Context:** image-only, page-local Track A; whole-report Track B; document aids explicitly logged; no models/predictions anywhere.
17. **Condition leakage:** opaque identifiers and stripped metadata; access control and direct API tests; visually unavoidable condition is acknowledged rather than mislabelled as blinding.
18. **Financial facts:** only metrics-driven fact layers; raw strings first; normalization is a frozen deterministic evaluation transform, not annotator improvisation.
19. **Provenance:** immutable identity/hash/index/user/protocol/tool/time/status/lineage fields listed in the method report.
20. **Tools for 100–600 pages:** custom POC and image-list Label Studio Community are feasible under synthetic conditions; neither is immediately production-ready. Prodigy needs a POC; doccano lacks relevant evidence.
21. **POC:** synthetic 600-page navigation and coordinate/export tests worked; LS failed blind visibility/provenance requirements; custom failed deployment authentication requirements.
22. **Unresolved:** B6 size/seed, B7/evaluator, exact normalization, ambiguity denominator, multiple MD&A primary rule, final tool/security, and all legacy route decisions.
23. **Author approvals:** every `RECOMMENDED_PENDING_AUTHOR_APPROVAL` or `UNRESOLVED` table row; especially the population/denominator choices that affect estimands.
24. **Direct protocol candidates:** record separation, page/document context split, source-aid separation, raw-record retention, task-specific agreement reporting, and no-engine-output blinding.
25. **Legacy dependencies:** route class taxonomy, route precedence, and OracleRouteGold evidence policy remain `PENDING` from the independent legacy research track.

## Required author choices before the next task

1. Approve/revise the PageGold text convention, empty/unreadable states, and metric-specific layers without changing frozen denominators silently.
2. Approve/revise B6: formal reliability population, independent selection seed, random overlap size, and rare-case diagnostic rule.
3. Approve/revise B7: atomic anchor definition, table treatment, pair eligibility, and the matching evaluator contract.
4. Decide DocumentGold's ambiguity/multiple-span primary-metric policy; do not force a span merely because the benchmark needs a number.
5. Choose a production tooling path and require a credential, provenance, leakage, and non-Gold real-document POC before human Gold begins.
6. Wait for T0.4-LF-RECON before considering B1/B2/B5.
