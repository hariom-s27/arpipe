## 1. B1 - `legacy_font` route

**OLD FROZEN RULE.** T0.4 registers `legacy_font` as one of five routing-metric classes (`configs/t0_4/metrics_spec.json`, `levels.ROUTING.classes`, with a `false_legacy_rate`), `LEGACY` as one of three oracle gold routes (`configs/t0_4/oracle_routing_spec.json`, `gold_routes`), and "Legacy-font remediation is a separate REMAP lane, not OCR" (preregistration, "Routing and legacy-font experiments"). The T0.4 closure audit (observation 2) states that a class-to-route map exists in T0.4 only for `vector_text`, and leaves `broken_text`/`legacy_font` to "the gold-phase protocol".

**PROBLEM / GAP.** Three registers name the concept and none joins them: the routing classes, the oracle routes and the REMAP lane. No artifact says `legacy_font` maps to `LEGACY`, that `LEGACY` is the REMAP lane, or that `legacy_font` differs from `broken_text`.

**EVIDENCE.**

| concept | registered detector class | registered route | source artifact | source commit | evidence | mapping_status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `legacy_font` (routing-metric class) | none: `PageKind` has six members (`digital`, `scanned`, `hybrid`, `broken_text`, `vector_text`, `blank`) and `_classify` never emits `legacy_font` | none: `NEEDS_OCR` = {scanned, broken_text, vector_text, hybrid}; no route table; no `remap` token anywhere in `arpipe/` | `arpipe/models.py`, `arpipe/triage.py`, `arpipe/CLAUDE.md` | `9cf5192`, `b467e36`, `0fb8cbf` | tests `test_b1_frozen_detector_has_no_legacy_font_class`, `test_b1_frozen_router_has_no_legacy_or_remap_route` | **LEGACY_ROUTE_REQUIRES_FURTHER_DEFINITION** |
| `legacy_font_candidate` (T0.4 manifest label) | `broken_text`, by construction: `cid_or_broken_text_indicator` is defined as `p["kind"] == "broken_text"`, and `core.py` turns it into the label | `OCR` (the detector's route for `broken_text`) | `tools/freeze_extraction_corpus.py`, `tools/t0_4/core.py`, `dataset/corpus_freeze/page_profile.csv` | `23d6228`, `b937b58` | 803 of 803 `broken_text` pages carry the indicator and no other kind does (37,917 profiled pages); in the 475-unit manifest the label and class `broken_text` coincide on 20 of 20 units, neither without the other | identical to `broken_text` in the frozen data; this is not a `legacy_font` route |
| `broken_text` | `broken_text` | `OCR`, "discard the text layer" | `arpipe/triage.py`, `arpipe/models.py`, `arpipe/CLAUDE.md` | `b467e36`, `9cf5192`, `0fb8cbf` | explicit in code and comments | CONFIRMED (detector route) |
| `LEGACY` (oracle gold route) | none | REMAP lane, described in prose only | `configs/t0_4/oracle_routing_spec.json`, preregistration | `b937b58`, `33ba76a` | no artifact defines `LEGACY`; `tools/t0_4/` is setup-only, with no dispatch; the corpus freeze excluded remapping (`freeze_protocol.md`) | undefined |

Three non-equivalent operationalisations of "legacy font" exist in the record, which is why no merger can be assumed:

1. **T0.4 page label** = `kind == broken_text` (803 pages), as above.
2. **T0.3A/T0.1R document-level `legacy_candidate_page_count`** counts a page when `is_t0_broken or U+FFFD count > 0 or PUA count > 5` (`tools/audit_corpus_gaps.py`, `b6b76d9`). It is a document-level count and is broader than (1).
3. **P-B3 font-evidence measure** (context only): legacy-font presence on 2,075 pages (5.47%), of which 78.3% have clean native text; 65.4% of corrupted pages have no legacy-font evidence (`ARPipe_Memory/experiments/P-B3.md`, thesis repo). Its report, `reports/legacy_font_corpus_measurement.md`, was never committed in the ARPipe repo, so it is **not** used as frozen evidence and the decision does not depend on it.

**DECISION.** `LEGACY_ROUTE_REQUIRES_FURTHER_DEFINITION`. `LEGACY_ROUTE_DEFINITION_BLOCKER` is set. No `legacy_font` mapping was created, the production detector is untouched, `legacy_font` was not merged with `broken_text`, and no detector was defined.

**SCIENTIFIC RATIONALE.** The frozen T0.4 design needs `legacy_font` to be a routable class, and the frozen record cannot supply that. (a) In `ACTUAL_ROUTING` the only detector cannot emit `legacy_font`, so the `false_legacy_rate` and the `legacy_font` row of the confusion matrix have no predictor. (b) The REMAP lane has no implementation, so `ORACLE_ROUTING` has nothing to route a `LEGACY` page to. (c) Reading `legacy_font` as `broken_text` would silently change five registered routing classes to four and contradict the frozen statement that remediation is not OCR. (d) Reading `legacy_font` as `LEGACY` would invent a detector. Each option is a design decision, not something the record already fixes.

**IMPACT ON T0.4.** None applied. Any resolution changes a frozen file (`metrics_spec.json` and/or `oracle_routing_spec.json`) or adds a new detector definition, so it needs your authorisation first (see section 4).

**WHAT REMAINS UNCHANGED.** Everything: all T0.4 configs, manifests, tools, tests and audits, the frozen corpus, and all of `arpipe/`.

## 2. B2 - route precedence

**OLD FROZEN RULE.** No routing precedence is registered. The only ordered list naming both concepts is `sampling_stratum_priority` in `configs/t0_4/sampling_spec.json` (`legacy_font_candidate` before `broken_text`). The spec scopes it to choosing one primary sampling stratum per page. That is why the manifest has no unit with stratum `broken_text` (all 20 fall to `legacy_font_candidate`).

**EVIDENCE.**

| class_1 | class_2 | can both apply to one page? | precedence | source artifact | source commit | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `legacy_font` (as the T0.4 label) | `broken_text` | yes, totally: identical page sets, 803 of 803 | none for routing; only the sampling-scoped priority | `sampling_spec.json`, `tools/t0_4/core.py` | `b937b58` | tests `test_b2_the_two_concepts_coincide_on_every_frozen_page`, `test_b2_only_a_sampling_scoped_ordering_names_both_concepts` |
| `broken_text` | `vector_text` | no: `_classify` gives `broken_text` only when `n_chars >= 120` and `vector_text` only when `n_chars < 120` | code order in `_classify` | `arpipe/triage.py` | `b467e36` | code read (context) |

**DECISION.** `ROUTE_PRECEDENCE_UNRESOLVED`. `NOT_APPLICABLE` is not available: B1 leaves `legacy_font` undefined, and under the only frozen operationalisation it is the same set of pages as `broken_text`, so the two can overlap with no routing rule to order them. `LEGACY > broken_text` was not adopted; the sampling priority was not reused as a routing rule.

**SCIENTIFIC RATIONALE.** A sampling priority answers "which stratum gets this page", not "which route processes it". Reusing it would turn a sampling convenience into a routing decision the record never made.

**IMPACT ON T0.4.** None applied. B2 dissolves if B1 is resolved by declaring one class or by defining `legacy_font` on independent evidence with its own stated precedence.

**WHAT REMAINS UNCHANGED.** As section 1.

B1 needs your decision first; the rest of the amendment cannot be decided before it. The options, each of which changes something frozen:

| Option | What it means | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **A. One class, OCR route** *(recommended)* | `legacy_font` is not a separate detector class; the frozen identity with `broken_text` is stated as the definition, and ACTUAL_ROUTING sends those pages to OCR as the detector already does | `metrics_spec.json` `ROUTING.classes` loses `legacy_font` (or keeps it as documented-empty); `oracle_routing_spec.json` `gold_routes` loses or narrows `LEGACY`; the REMAP lane becomes a separately registered experiment |
| B. Oracle-only `LEGACY` | `LEGACY` stays a human-verified oracle label with no detector counterpart; ACTUAL_ROUTING never predicts it | needs a written `LEGACY` definition, a B5 evidence policy that admits font/text-layer diagnostics, and a REMAP implementation before the oracle arm can run |

I recommend A because it is the only option the frozen data already supports (the two are the same 803 pages, and the detector has one route for them), it needs no invented detector or lane, and it keeps the routing metrics computable. It does narrow the registered five-class routing design, which is why it needs your authorisation. P-B3 (context only, uncommitted report) suggests font evidence and text corruption diverge, so a genuine REMAP experiment will eventually need option C's definition; A only defers it cleanly.
