## 7–13. What conditions exist (full detail in `diversity_matrix.csv`)

**PDF technical conditions** (doc-level, from `documents.jsonl` + a fresh per-document open/hash/font/geometry pass): `pdf_producer` spans at least 20 distinct tools across three eras — modern Office/online tools (`Microsoft® Word 2016/2019/for Microsoft 365`, `iLovePDF`/`ilovepdf.com`, 21+3 docs), professional publishing tools (`Adobe PDF Library 9.9/10.0.1/15.0/17.0`, `Acrobat Distiller 6.0/7.0/10.0.0`), OCR/scan tools (`ABBYY FineReader 11` — 6 docs, `OmniPageCSDK18` — 3 docs), and generic print-to-PDF tools (`Microsoft: Print To PDF`, `CutePDF Demo`). All 194 are unencrypted (both `documents.jsonl`'s recorded flag and a fresh `pymupdf` re-check agree — **0 conflicts**). Page counts range 1–661 (mean ≈195). PDF format strings observed span `PDF 1.3` through `PDF 1.7` (from the fresh per-document open pass).

**Text-layer conditions** (from `profiles.jsonl`, produced by the base commit's own `triage`/`textlayer` stages — `KNOWN_FROM_PRIOR`): page kinds across ~37.9k pages are `digital` (35,580), `two/three-column` variety (see below), `scanned` (804 pages / 5 documents / 5 issuers), `broken_text` (803 pages / 24 documents / 15 issuers — the mojibake/legacy-CID-font symptom this freeze calls `legacy_font`), `hybrid` (483 pages), `blank` (215 pages), `vector_text` (32 pages / 20 documents / 15 issuers — glyphs drawn as vector paths rather than encoded text). Document-level `doc_kind` (already computed by the base commit's pipeline): 151 `native`/digital, 38 `mixed`, 5 fully `scanned`.

**Language/script conditions**: `profiles.jsonl`'s page-level `script` field is `latin` for 37,177 of ~37.9k pages and `unknown`/`mixed` for the rest — **zero** pages are tagged `devanagari` anywhere in the corpus, and the doc-level `bilingual` flag is `False` for all 194 documents (`KNOWN_FROM_PRIOR`). A fresh, independent check in this run (regex `[\u0900-\u097f]` over a sampled page set per document) found actual Devanagari text in **2 documents, both belonging to a single issuer** (`INE002L01015`) — the only non-Latin-script evidence anywhere in the local corpus. This is consistent with, not contradicted by, `PIPELINE_STATUS.md`'s independent note that Hindi OCR support is not installed on this machine.

**Layout conditions**: two-column pages appear in 189/194 documents (36/36 issuers); 3+-column pages in 188/194 documents. Only 5 documents (across 1 issuer's worth of concentration, spread otherwise) show zero multi-column pages. Mixed page rotation within a single document occurs in 23 documents (13 issuers) — a real "mixed orientation" condition, not fabricated. A fresh mediabox pass found 156/194 documents contain more than one distinct page size.

**Structure conditions**: PDF outlines (bookmarks) are present in 90/194 documents (34 issuers) and absent in 104 (32 issuers) — this freeze treats PDF-outline presence and "bookmark presence" as the same signal (they are the same PDF-spec object). Outline depth ≥3 distinct levels ("deep hierarchy") occurs in 28 documents across 15 issuers.

**Heading conditions**: of the 90 documents with an outline, 8 have at least one outline entry whose title contains "discussion" (`mda_heading_in_outline`). Of those, exactly 2 documents (1 issuer, `INE009A01021`) have an outline title matching a discussion-like heading that is **not** the canonical "Management Discussion and Analysis" wording — flagged `unusual_heading`. 5 documents (2 issuers) have an outline entry containing "annexure". **0** documents show the `combined_mda` heuristic (a discussion-titled entry that also mentions "governance"/"annexure"/"report on" in the same title) or the `semantic_impostor` heuristic (≥2 discussion-like outline entries in one document) — both are legitimate zero counts for this corpus, not unmeasured; their evidence tag in `diversity_matrix.csv` is `INFERRED`, not `UNKNOWN`, because the underlying outline-text scan *was* run for every document that has an outline.

## What this freeze does not claim

Per section 35 of the T0 brief: this corpus is **not** claimed to be representative of all Indian annual reports or of any 576-company universe referenced elsewhere in this project; not every PDF condition is covered (see the `UNKNOWN` list above); rare conditions are not guaranteed to recur in HOLDOUT (two are confined to a single issuer/split, reported explicitly in §15–16); MD&A presence/absence is not gold-standard anywhere in this freeze; text-layer diagnosis, reading order, heading detection, span resolution, and the extractor's accuracy are untouched by this task — no accuracy result of any kind is produced or implied here.
